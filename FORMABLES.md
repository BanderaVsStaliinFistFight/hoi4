# Formable nations

_Generated by `tools/generate.py` from `tools/formables.json`._

Each decision costs political power and needs the listed states to be owned and controlled by you or one of your subjects, while you are independent, not capitulated and not in a civil war. Proclaiming a nation annexes your subjects that belong to it, cores every state of its territory you own, claims the rest, and grants **Euphoria of Unification** (+10% stability, +5% war support, +10% political power gain for one year).
